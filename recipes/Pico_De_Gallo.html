Pico De Gallo

Ingredients:

  • Tomatos
  • White Onions
  • Cilantro
  • Jalapeño
  • 1 Lime
  • Salt
  • 2 Garlic

Recipe:

  1. Dice tomatos/onions/jalapeños, chop cilantro and mix in bowl
  2. Add lime juice/salt and stir
  3. Cover bowl and place in fridge for 30-60 minutes
  4. Remove from fridge and serve
