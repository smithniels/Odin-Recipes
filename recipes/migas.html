(Mexican Scrambled Eggs)

Ingredients:

  • Eggs 8
  • Heavy Cream 1/4 cup
  • Salt 1/2 tsp
  • Black Pepper 1/4 tsp
  • Taco Seasoning 1 tsp
  • Queso Fresco 1 cup
  • Red Onion 1
  • Red Bell Pepper 1

Recipe:

  1. Prepare Ingredients in a bowl: Break open 8 eggs, Add 2 tablespoons of heavy cream, add 1/2 tsp salt and 1/4 black pepper.
  2. Beat well for one minute or until eggs are not lumpy. SET ASIDE.
  3. In a wide skillet, heat 2 tablespoons of oil. Add diced veggies (onion, bell pepper, & jalapeños) with taco seasoning.
  4. Sauté peppers until softened. (3-4 minutes)
  5. Add broken torilla chips and mix with veggies for 1 minute
  6. Pour in the egg mixture and cook on medium heat. Stir occasionally until eggs are cooked through. (5-7 minutes)
  7. Serve with Avocados or salsa

Migas can also be put into tortillas for breakfast tacos!
