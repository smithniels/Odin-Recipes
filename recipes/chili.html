Ingredients:

    (for 8 servings)

  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • 8 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 red bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 jalapeño, chopped/li>
  • 1 teaspoon salt, to taste
  • ¼ teaspoon pepper, to taste
  • 1 tablespoon cayenne pepper
  • 4 tablespoons chili powder
  • 1 tablespoon cumin
  • 4 tomatoes, cubed
  • 28 oz crushed tomato(795 g), 1 can
  • 4 cups vegetable broth(960 mL)
  • 2 cups water(480 mL)
  • 1 ½ cups quinoa(255 g), rinsed
  • 1 cup red kidney bean(175 g), drained
  • 1 cup pinto bean(175 g), drained
  • 1 cup black beans(170 g), drained
  • 1 cup corn(175 g), fresh or frozen
  • 1 tablespoon lime juice
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 tablespoon fresh cilantro

Recipe:

  1. n a large pot, over medium heat, combine oil, garlic, onion, pepper, jalapeño, salt, pepper, cayenne pepper, chili powder, and cumin.
  2. Sauté until onion is translucent, 5-6 minutes.
  3. Add tomatoes, crushed tomatoes, vegetable broth, water, quinoa, kidney beans, pinto beans, and black beans.
  4. Bring to a boil.
  5. Cover and reduce to a simmer for 25-30 minutes.
  6. Add corn, lime juice, oregano, and cilantro, cover again and simmer for 5 minutes.
  7. Allow to cool 2 minutes. Serve topped with avocado and cilantro.
